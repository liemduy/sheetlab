# V0.2 Checkpoint 11: Browser QA

## Goal

Verify the V0.2 notation editor in the in-app browser as a real user would: load the app, inspect the visible sheet, place a note, and switch to grand staff.

## Acceptance Criteria

- The local app loads at `http://localhost:5173/`.
- Treble staff renders with VexFlow notation.
- Clicking the visible staff places an event.
- Placed events appear in both the overlay hit layer and the VexFlow-rendered notation layer.
- Switching to grand staff renders treble and bass staff systems.
- Browser console has no runtime errors during the checked flow.
- Any browser-discovered blocking UI issue is fixed before checkpoint.

## Browser QA Result

- Loaded `http://localhost:5173/` successfully.
- Confirmed main editor shell, toolbar, V0.2 surface note, and treble notation system.
- Confirmed initial treble score renders 4 VexFlow staves.
- Clicked the visible staff and confirmed:
  - 1 overlay `score-event`.
  - 1 VexFlow `.sheetlab-vf-event`.
  - status changed to `Event placed`.
- Switched score type to `grand` and confirmed:
  - 1 `Grand staff notation system` overlay.
  - 1 bass staff overlay guide.
  - 8 VexFlow staves for 4 measures across treble and bass.
- Confirmed browser error log count: 0.

## Issue Found And Fixed

The in-app browser viewport exposed a responsive layout problem: the settings panel and sticky toolbar made the sheet difficult to reach on narrow windows.

Fix:

- Raised the responsive workspace breakpoint to `1100px`.
- Ordered the sheet surface before the settings panel on narrower layouts.
- Disabled sticky topbar in the narrower layout so the sheet is not covered while scrolling.
