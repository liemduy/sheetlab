# v0.6 Step 05 - Ledger Range

## Goal

Make editor and PDF ledger lines robust enough for real piano/sheet entry, with
space above and below the staff so ledger notes are not clipped.

## Result

- Increased editor staff vertical space for treble and grand staff surfaces.
- Expanded hover/editable vertical range so users can reach deeper ledger notes.
- Grand-staff hit testing now chooses the nearest staff when editable bands
  overlap, avoiding treble/bass ambiguity.
- Added ledger stress coverage for:
  - treble F3, A3, C4, A5, C6, E6
  - bass C1, A1, C2, C4, E4, G4
- Editor and PDF tests verify ledger line counts and unclipped drawing bounds.
