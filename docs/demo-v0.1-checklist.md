# SheetLab V0.1 Demo Checklist

## Demo Flow

1. Open `http://localhost:5173`.
2. Select `Treble only`.
3. Hover the staff and confirm the hover state updates.
4. Click the staff to place several notes.
5. Change duration and accidental on the selected note.
6. Use Undo and Redo.
7. Press Play and confirm the playhead/highlight appears.
8. Save, Reset, then Load.
9. Download JSON.
10. Export PDF through the print dialog.
11. Switch to `Grand staff piano` and confirm two staves render.

## Pass Criteria

- No runtime crash during the demo flow.
- Staff rendering remains visible after edits.
- Event count changes after placement/delete/load.
- Playback can start and stop.
- Save/load restores the placed score event.
- Print/export path opens the browser print dialog.
